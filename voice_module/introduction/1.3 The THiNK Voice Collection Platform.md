# 1.3 The THiNK Voice Collection Platform

The **Voice Collection App** is a practical, open-source tool for community-driven voice data collection. It has already been deployed successfully in Kenya, most notably through the *Africa Next Voices* project, an initiative supported by the **Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation**. This project demonstrated the feasibility of mobilising communities at scale to contribute voice data in underrepresented African languages, ensuring that technological advancements are inclusive and locally relevant.

The app is publicly available on the Google Play Store: **Voice Collection App**.
The **voice data collected is open-source and available on Hugging Face**, enabling researchers, developers, and communities to freely access and build upon it.

Importantly, the platform also functions as a **gig platform** — contributors are **paid for recording and submitting their voices**, creating new income opportunities while advancing inclusive AI.

---

## Key Features of the Platform

- **Mobile-first design:** Built for Android devices, ensuring accessibility across Kenya where smartphones are increasingly prevalent.  
- **Offline capability:** Contributions can be recorded without internet access and uploaded when connectivity is available.  
- **Consent-first approach:** Contributors are guided through simple, multilingual scripts that ensure informed participation.  
- **Flexible datasets:** Data can be exported in standard formats (e.g., WAV, JSON, XML) for use in research and natural language processing (NLP).  
- **Inclusive design:** Interfaces are multilingual, with localised prompts that make participation intuitive.  

---

## Alignment with Digital Public Goods (DPG)

The Voice Collection App meets the criteria of a **Digital Public Good (DPG):**

- **Open-source and transparent:** Freely available for use, adaptation, and improvement by communities worldwide.  
- **Privacy and data sovereignty:** Contributors’ identities are protected, with anonymisation and encryption standards in place.  
- **Interoperability:** Designed to integrate with global language technology efforts, reducing duplication and fragmentation.  
- **Equity-driven:** Tailored to low-resource contexts, ensuring that underrepresented voices are included in the digital revolution.  

---

## Target Languages for Initial Deployment

The *Africa Next Voices* project and subsequent deployments have prioritised a set of culturally significant and underrepresented African languages, including:

- **Kikuyu** (Kenya)  
- **Dholuo** (Kenya)  
- **Maasai** (Kenya/Tanzania)  
- **Kalenjin** (Kenya)  
- **Somali** (Kenya, Somalia, Ethiopia)
