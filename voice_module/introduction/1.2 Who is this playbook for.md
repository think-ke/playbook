# 1.2 Who is this Playbook For?

This Playbook is intended for a diverse audience of stakeholders who play a role in shaping inclusive voice technologies. While the focus is on grassroots and community-driven initiatives, its scope extends across technical, academic, and policy domains.

## Community Leaders and NGOs
Designed for organisations working directly with local populations, especially in rural and low-resource settings. The Playbook provides practical methods for mobilising participants, securing consent, and maintaining trust.

## Developers and Innovators
Technology practitioners, start-ups, and open-source contributors can use the Playbook to understand data collection standards, integrate open-source toolkits, and build scalable platforms for voice technologies.

## Researchers and Academics
Linguists, computer scientists, and social scientists can rely on the Playbook for methodological guidance, ethical frameworks, and case studies that enrich comparative and applied research.

## Funders and Policymakers
Donors, governments, and institutions can engage with the Playbook to gain insight into the long-term value of supporting language technology, as well as strategies for scaling and sustaining initiatives.

## Communities and Contributors
Individuals who participate in voice recording, annotation, or validation will find guidance on their rights, responsibilities, and opportunities to shape the future of language technology.

By addressing these groups collectively, the Playbook fosters collaboration across sectors to achieve inclusive and equitable outcomes.
