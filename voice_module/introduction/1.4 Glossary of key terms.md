# 4. Glossary of Key Terms

- **Annotation:** The process of labelling data, often including transcription or translation of recorded speech.  
- **Corpus (plural: Corpora):** A structured set of language data (text or audio) used for linguistic and computational analysis.  
- **Data Sovereignty:** The right of communities to govern and control their data, including how it is collected, stored, and used.  
- **Low-resource language:** A language lacking sufficient digital resources for natural language processing applications.  
- **Metadata:** Descriptive information attached to a dataset (e.g., speaker’s age, gender, or region).  
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** A branch of artificial intelligence that enables machines to interpret and generate human language.  
- **Speech Recognition:** The process of converting spoken language into written text.  
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** The conversion of written text into spoken voice output.  
- **Speech-to-text (STT) :** The conversion of voice to written text.
- **Validation:** The process of reviewing and confirming the quality and accuracy of data collected.
