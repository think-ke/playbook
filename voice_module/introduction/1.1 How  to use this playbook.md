# 1.1 How to Use this Playbook

This Playbook is not intended to be read as a traditional book from start to finish. Instead, it is organised into standalone modules, each covering a specific dimension of voice data collection and use. Users may approach the Playbook in different ways depending on their roles and objectives.

## For Community Leaders and NGOs
- Begin with the **Preparation & Stakeholder Engagement** and **Governance & Ethics** modules.  
- Use the **Capacity Building** chapter to design workshops and training for local participants.  
- Refer to the **Pilot Projects** section for real-world examples that can be adapted locally.  

## For Developers and Innovators
- Focus on the **Data Collection Framework** and **Open Data Infrastructure** modules.  
- Engage with the **Toolkit** section to integrate open-source components into local projects.  
- Explore the **Interoperability** guidance to connect datasets with existing global repositories.  

## For Researchers and Academics
- Refer to the **Impact Metrics** module for frameworks on dataset evaluation.  
- Examine the **Governance & Ethics** guidelines to align with ethical standards.  
- Use case studies from **Pilot Projects** to inform comparative research.  

## For Funders and Policymakers
- Read the **Vision & Purpose** and **Sustainability & Scaling** sections.  
- Review the **Roadmap (3-Year Plan)** for a clear implementation pathway.  
- Consult **Impact Metrics** to identify areas for investment and accountability.
