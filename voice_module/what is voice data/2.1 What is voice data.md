# Chapter 2: What is Voice Data?

## Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces the concept of **voice data**: what it is, why it matters, and how it is used in practice. It explores:

- The different types of voice data  
- The characteristics of high-quality datasets  
- The ethical dimensions of voice collection  

A real-world example is drawn from the **Africa Next Voices** project, which deployed the **Voice Collection App** with the support of the **Africa Next Voices Project**.

---

## By the end of this chapter, readers will:

- Understand the definition and scope of voice data.  
- Recognise its importance for speech technologies and social inclusion.  
- Be able to distinguish between different types of voice datasets.  
- Appreciate the qualities of high-value voice data.  
- Be aware of the ethical and cultural issues surrounding data collection.
