## 2.2 Why Voice Data Matters

Voice data is the foundation for technologies that are increasingly shaping global communication.

---

### Applications of Voice Data

| Application               | Purpose                                | Impact for Low-Resource Languages |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)** | Converts speech into text.             | Enables voice typing and transcription tools in Kikuyu or Maasai. |
| **Text-to-Speech (TTS)**  | Converts text into natural-sounding speech. | Produces educational materials or audiobooks in Somali. |
| **Voice Assistants**      | Enables interaction with devices using speech. | Provides access to services in rural areas via simple voice commands. |
| **Language Preservation** | Archives oral traditions and endangered languages. | Preserves Maasai proverbs and Dholuo folk stories for future generations. |

---

***Voice data is therefore both a **technical necessity** and a **cultural resource*****
