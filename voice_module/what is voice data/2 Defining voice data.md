## 2.1 Defining Voice Data

**Voice data** is the digital capture of human speech, encompassing not only audio recordings but also their accompanying transcriptions, annotations, and contextual metadata that together create a comprehensive linguistic resource.

---

### Components of Voice Data

| Component           | Description                                                                 | Example                                                                                   |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Acoustic features** | The sound properties of speech, including pitch, intonation, rhythm, and pronunciation patterns | Rising tone in a Kikuyu question vs. flat tone in a statement |
| **Linguistic content** | The actual words and phrases spoken, requiring accurate transcription     | A Somali speaker reading traditional poetry aloud |
| **Paralinguistic cues** | Non-verbal vocal elements such as pauses, emphasis, emotion, and speech patterns | A Dholuo speaker's hesitation patterns during storytelling |
| **Contextual metadata** | Information about the speaker, recording environment, and collection circumstances | Gender: Female; Region: Rift Valley; County: Transnzoia; Age: 35; Device: Android smartphone; Setting: Indoor |
