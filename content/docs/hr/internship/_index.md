# Tech Innovators Network

## Proposal: Internship Program  
*For Students and Undergraduates*

Tech Innovators Network offers internship opportunities designed to develop essential skills and prepare participants for success in the workforce.

---

## Program Objectives

- **Practical exposure** to IT projects  
- **Development** of technical and professional skills  
- **Nurturing talent** for future employment  

---

## Student Internship Program

### Eligibility

- Enrolled in a **Bachelor’s/Master’s** program in IT or a related field  
- Basic knowledge of IT/programming concepts  

### Duration

- **3–6 months**  
- Part-time/full-time  
- Flexible with academic schedules  

### Features

- Mentored practical project assignments  
- Training on industry tools (e.g., cloud computing)  
- Networking opportunities with IT professionals  
- Remuneration  

### Requirements

- Attachment letter from your institution  
- Insurance from your institution  

---

## Undergraduate Internship Program

### Eligibility

- Recent graduates (within 1 year) in IT or a related field  
- Good background in programming, cloud computing, or a related field  

### Duration

- **6–12 months**  
- Part-time/full-time  
- Potential for conversion to permanent roles  

### Features

- Mentored practical project assignments  
- Training on industry tools (e.g., cloud computing)  
- Performance-based evaluations  
- Networking opportunities with IT professionals  
- Remuneration  

### Requirements

- Graduation certificate  

---

## Internship Roles & Departments

- **Software Development**
- **Data Science & AI**
- **Cybersecurity**
- **Cloud Computing**
- **DevOps & IT Operations**

---

## Recruitment Process

1. **Application Submission**  
   Submit CV and cover letter.
2. **Evaluation**  
   Review of academic background and skills.
3. **Interview**
4. **Offer & Onboarding**
