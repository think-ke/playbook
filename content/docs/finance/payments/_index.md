
    1. **PAYMENTS**
    2. **Purpose**

The purpose of this section is to guide all payments made by THiNK during the payment process. THiNK Director together with the Accounts Department endeavors to ensure that:

- THiNK reimburses for properly authorized goods, services or works that were received by THiNK.
- THiNK funds are being used efficiently as possible.
- Every payment is precise and sufficiently backed up.
    1. **Scope**

This section is to be used for all payments for goods, services and works for THINK.

- 1. **Responsibilities**

The accountant is in charge of the accounts unit as well as the execution and upkeep of the paperwork associated with this process.

The head of accounts units at THiNK shall confirm all the expenditures to be incurred by their units. The Director will approve all expenditures authorized by the accounts unit heads.

**3.4 Payments by Cheque**

1. Cheques are to be used for large payments where electronic transfer is not available.
2. All cheques should be pre-approved, properly recorded and well stored.
3. Cheques are approved for the following payments:

- Vendor payments
- Consultant and contractor fees
- Rent, utilities and recurring payments
- Refunds for approved employee expenses

1. Cheques are not to be used for the following payments:

- Loans
- Payroll
- Personal payments
- Transactions without proper documentation

1. Cheques are to be issued after following the required issuance process.
2. Supporting documents e.g. invoices, contracts are required before issuance of any cheque.
3. Bank reconciliation is to be done monthly by the accounts department.
4. Stop payments are to be done on lost cheques immediately.
5. Disciplinary action may be taken on unauthorized issuing of cheques.
6. Fraudulent cheques will lead to legal consequences.
7. Post-dated cheques are to be issued only with the director’s approval.

**3.5 Payments by Electronic Funds Transfer**

1. EFT are approved for the following payments:

- Payroll
- Loan payments
- Large vendor payments
- Consultant and contractor fees
- International remittances

1. All EFT transactions are to be accompanied by supporting documents.
2. Bank reconciliation are to be done monthly by the accountant.

**3.5 Payments by Cash**

1. Cash payments should be made as minimal as possible.
2. There should be proper documentation for cash payments with approval.
3. Any cash transaction exceeding 5,000 Kes would require approval.
4. Cash payments may be used for:

- Refunds to employees upon submission of receipts.
- Replenishment of the petty cash kit.
- One-time purchases where cash was the only option.
- Payment to vendors where a bank transfer was not possible (Urgent)

1. Cash payments may not be used for:

- Salaries
- Large vendor bills- a bank transfer is more lenient
- Transactions exceeding 5,000 Kes.

1. All cash payments should follow the approval process i.e. make a request to accounts unit, accounts department ensures compliance then disburses the cash.
2. The following payments by cash would require documentation:

- Employee refunds- employees are supposed to submit receipts for refunds before any disbursement.
- Payment to vendors- cash transactions with vendors would require an invoice or receipt.

1. All cash payments are to be recorded in the company ledger and transactions reconciled monthly.
2. Cash is to be stored in a secure area with limited access.
3. No unauthorized cash transaction will be allowed.
4. Unauthorized cash transactions may result in disciplinary actions.

**3.6 Petty Cash Management**

1. To cover cash payments, petty cash reserves is to be 50,000 per month.
2. The Accounts Unit is to receive a properly completed purchase requisition that has been approved by the Director prior to any petty cash disbursement.
3. Employees are to make a requisition for cash from the petty cash kit through the Accountant.
4. The accountant is to verify requisitions and distribute cash.
5. Prior to the funds being released, the Director is to approve the expenditure.
6. When the funds run low, the custodian is to submit proof of previous expenditure to the director and make a request for replenishment.
7. All expenses must be supported by a receipt.
8. Where a receipt is missing, staff are to provide a written explanation justifying the expense or proof of payment.
9. An unsubstantiated expense may be deducted from the employee’s salary.
10. The Petty cash file will serve as supporting documentation containing all receipts and payments made from the Petty Cash Kit.
11. Petty cash is to be reconciled monthly to audit and ensure compliance.
12. Petty cash is not to be used for the following expenses:
    - Personal expense such as fare to and from the office.
    - Large purchases unless authorized.
    - A salary advance or loan.
